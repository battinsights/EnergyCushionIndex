EPRINC Oil and Gas Cushion Index

Published by the Energy Policy Research Foundation

Loading Download data Methodology

How much physical buffer the United States and global oil and natural gas system holds against an unexpected supply loss.

The index runs from 0 to 100. Higher means more slack. A score of 50 is the calibrated adequate level, not the historical average. Scores below 40 are classified Tight or Strained. Updated monthly as new data is published.
